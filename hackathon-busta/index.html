Payroll and labour admin for founders hiring their first 1–20 people

Run payroll without the paperwork, and get an answer the same day.

Busta works out every euro a hire really costs. It sets the money aside for salaries, F24 taxes and severance (TFR) before each deadline. When you have a question, an AI assistant answers in seconds and a licensed labour consultant (consulente del lavoro) checks anything it can't settle.
